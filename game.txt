# --- GAME CORE --- #
A lucid dreamer trapped in a looping gas station must choose between trusting a cold interdimensional agency 
or facing a parasitic entity alone as reality collapses around him.


Set in the NOIA universe
NOIA monitors and stabilizes anomalies in the Oniric Space

# --- PREMISE --- #

The entire game takes place inside a lucid dream.

The player is a lucid dreamer stuck in a looping gas station environment.
The dreamer forced this loop upon himself to stay safe in the first place.
Because of his lucidity, he leaves a “trail” in the dream space.

A parasitic entity that uses trails like this to travel between realities is hunting the player to gain access to another reality.

NOIA detects the instability but struggles to reach him.

The gas station becomes a distorted battleground between:

- The Dreamer (player)

- The Parasite

- NOIA

# --- STRUCTURE --- #

= PHASE 1 – Simulation =

Player performs mundane gas station tasks:

- Fueling cars

- Taking out trash

- Cleaning [Maybe]

- Stocking [Maybe]

- Punching in/out

- Handling customers [Maybe]

Everything feels normal at first.


= PHASE 2 – Anomalies Begin =

Subtle distortions appear:

Trash reappears after being taken out

Cars all look identical

Time inconsistencies

Repeated customers

Doors leading to slightly altered interiors

Sound desyncs

Flickering lights that don't match switches

Objects in wrong positions

! Player logs anomalies on their phone.

! Anomalies escalate as the parasite gets closer.



= PHASE 3 – Contact =

All activity suddenly stops.

The TV turns on.

NOIA speaks:

They warn the player that something is coming.
They are trying everything in their power to reach him but it is very difficult.

At this point. The creature itself appears and the tasks of the player switch. He now has to survive.



= PHASE 4 – The Choice =

NOIA says they cannot isolate his location.

Player choice:

Option A – Trust NOIA
Tell NOIA about the anomaly log

→ Mission: Climb signal tower
→ Plug phone into beacon
→ NOIA isolates and reaches him

Option B – Don't trust NOIA
Say Nothing

→ NOIA cannot locate him
→ Reality worsens
→ Gas station collapses into nightmare space


# --- CORE LOOP (Early Game) --- #

Work
Notice anomaly
Log anomaly
Repeat

Tension builds slowly through the anomalies 


# --- CORE LOOP (Late Game) --- #

Survive
Repair
Avoid creature
Make decision
